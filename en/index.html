About

I see the light.
I command the set.

Hi, I am Diego. A Gaffer with over 13 years of experience lighting feature films and commercials — blending creative visual intuition with the rigorous technical precision demanded on set, from initial lighting concepts to the final trim before rolling.

I am also a co-founder of WarmWhite, specialized in advanced dynamic lighting control for film sets, and Aural Stage, dedicated to virtual production and LED volume lighting integration with Unreal Engine — pioneering next-generation in-camera visual lighting in Spain.

Proprietary Project

WarmWhite.

Alongside my work as a gaffer, I am a co-founder of WarmWhite, a specialized venture dedicated to on-set luminaire control via DMX.

The goal: patching, programming, and operating all on-set lighting from a single, unified system — engineered to streamline the electrical crew's workflow while ensuring precise, granular control for every scene.
